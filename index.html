CONTROLS

ON FOOT (DESKTOP)

WASD — Move
Mouse — Look
Shift — Sprint
Space — Jump
E — Interact / Enter vehicle
LMB — Shoot
R — Reload
M — Minimap mode
Tab — Full map
Esc — Pause

ON FOOT (MOBILE)

Left stick — Move
Drag right side — Look
SPRINT button — Sprint
JUMP button — Jump
INTERACT button — Interact
SHOOT button — Shoot

GENERAL

F1 — FPS counter
Esc — Pause
